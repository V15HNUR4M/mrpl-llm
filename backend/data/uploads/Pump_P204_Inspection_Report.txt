PUMP P204 INSPECTION REPORT
Asset: Pump P204
Inspection Date: 2026-08-18

GENERAL CONDITION
Pump P204 was operational during inspection. The casing was intact with no visible external cracks. Minor oil staining was observed around the bearing housing.

BEARING CONDITION
The drive-end bearing showed elevated vibration compared with the normal operating condition. A faint abnormal bearing noise was also reported. Bearing lubrication should be checked and the bearing condition monitored closely.

SEAL CONDITION
Minor leakage was observed near the mechanical seal. The leakage was not severe enough to require immediate shutdown, but the seal should be inspected during the next planned maintenance window.

VIBRATION
Measured vibration was higher than the normal baseline at the drive end. Possible causes include bearing wear, coupling misalignment, or imbalance.

TEMPERATURE
Bearing temperature was elevated but remained below the alarm threshold during the inspection.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Inspect and lubricate the drive-end bearing.
- Check coupling alignment.
- Inspect the mechanical seal for degradation.
- Continue monitoring vibration and bearing temperature.
- Escalate for corrective maintenance if vibration or temperature continues to increase.

Overall assessment: Pump P204 can remain in service with increased monitoring, subject to maintenance follow-up.
